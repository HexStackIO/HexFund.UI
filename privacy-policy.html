HexFund Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

HexFund ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the HexFund mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide our services:

  • Account information (email address, name) provided during registration
  • Financial data you enter into the app (transactions, account balances, categories)
  • Authentication tokens issued by Microsoft Entra for secure sign-in
  • Feedback you choose to send from the app (bug reports, ideas and questions)
  • Optional diagnostics sent with a feedback report, only if you turn them on: app version, build number, device model, operating system version, the screen you were using, the app theme, and a short list of recent failed requests (method, a generic request path without any of your data, status or error type, and a reference number)

Diagnostics never include your email address, name, account names, amounts, transaction descriptions, category names or sign-in tokens.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide the HexFund service, including:

  • Authenticating your identity and maintaining your session
  • Storing and displaying your financial planning data
  • Improving app performance and reliability
  • Reviewing the feedback you send and following up with you when needed

The text of a feedback report is analyzed by Anthropic's Claude to categorize and summarize it.

Feedback Reports

When you send feedback, the report is stored in our database and then copied into a private issue in a maintainer-only GitHub repository. The issue carries an opaque reference number and does not contain your email address, name or user ID. The link between a report and you exists only in our database and is used only to follow up with you.

The diagnostics switch on the feedback screen is off by default. You can see exactly what will be sent before you submit, and you choose whether to include it for each report. Please do not type sensitive personal details into a feedback message.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in Microsoft Azure infrastructure located in the United States. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal information. Your financial data is never shared with advertisers or data brokers. We use the following service providers to operate HexFund: Microsoft (sign-in and hosting), GitHub (private issues for feedback reports) and Anthropic (analysis of feedback text).

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and your data at any time from the app (Settings, then Delete My Account); see our account deletion page. Deleting your account removes your financial data, your profile, your sign-in identity and your feedback records from our database.

Feedback records in our database are also deleted automatically 12 months after they are created. The text of a feedback report may remain in the private issue as a de-identified product record, because the issue does not contain information that identifies you. If you want a report removed, contact us and we will redact and close the issue.

Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including secure authentication via Microsoft Entra External ID and encrypted data transmission.

Children's Privacy

HexFund is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hexstack@outlook.com. See also our support page.

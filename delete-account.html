Delete your
account & data

You have the right to permanently remove your HexFund account and all associated financial data from our systems. This page explains how.

In-App Process

How to delete your account

  1. 01 Open the HexFund app on your device and sign in.
  2. 02 Tap your profile avatar in the top-right corner to open your account menu.
  3. 03 Navigate to Settings → Account.
  4. 04 Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. 05 Review the confirmation screen and tap Confirm Deletion to proceed. This action cannot be undone.
Deletion Timeline

What happens to your data

Permanently deleted within 30 days
  • All transaction records and financial entries you have created
  • Your account profile and personal preferences
  • Any categories, budgets, or custom configurations
  • Your authentication link to Microsoft Entra ID
  • All locally cached data on your device
Important Notice

Before you delete

  • Deletion is permanent and irreversible — your data cannot be recovered after the 30-day window closes.
  • HexFund does not connect to bank accounts, so no external accounts are affected.
  • Deleting your HexFund account does not delete your Microsoft account.
  • You may re-register at any time, but your previous data will not be restored.
Can't access the app?

If you are unable to access HexFund to complete the deletion yourself, you may submit a request by emailing privacy@hexstack.io from the address associated with your account. Include your full name and registered email address. We will process your request within 30 days.

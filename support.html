HexFund Support

Need help with HexFund, or want to tell us about a problem or an idea? We would like to hear from you.

Send feedback from the app

If you are signed in, open Settings and tap Send feedback. You can report a bug, share an idea or ask a question. You choose whether to include optional technical details, and you can see exactly what is sent before you submit.

Contact us by email

If you cannot sign in or the feedback form is not available, email us at hexstack@outlook.com. Please include the app version (shown in Settings) and a short description of what happened.

HexFund is in beta. Replies are best effort.
